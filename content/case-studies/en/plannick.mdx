## The challenge

Professional event management is often spread across spreadsheets, messages, documents and several different tools. This fragmentation makes it difficult to understand the true state of each project, coordinate responsibilities and keep the client informed.

The challenge was to turn that scattered process into a structured digital experience that is simple to use and flexible enough for both independent professionals and growing teams.

## The solution

I designed and built Plannick as an operational workspace for managing the entire event lifecycle, from the arrival of a commercial opportunity through planning and delivery.

The platform brings together the lead pipeline, active events, phases, tasks, owners, payments, suppliers, guests and important dates. It also includes a secure portal where each client can review progress, respond to decisions and access relevant information without relying on constant follow-ups by email or WhatsApp.

## The outcome

The result is a platform designed to replace manual processes with a clearer, more consistent and scalable way of working. The team gains a complete view of the operation while the client receives a more transparent and professional experience.

Plannick demonstrates how a complex operational need can become a useful, intuitive digital product that is ready to grow.
